Escaping the price war.

Your exclusive brand offerings sold only at Magnolia Road, targeted at a specific customer segment to provide value vs your competitors.

Current tactic

Carry the brands everyone carries

  • Summit, Dro and the rest are stocked by most dispensaries in the state
  • All packaged brands are in conflict with their retail partners financial success
  • The retail store builds the vendor's brand, at their own expense!
What it costs you

Nobody wins a race to the bottom

  • Customer can EASILY price-comparison shop
  • Whoever discounts hardest this week sets your ceiling and takes the sale
  • You win only on the days you are the cheapest, and the margin goes with it

You cannot discount your way to safety

The goal

Create captive customers

  • Two exclusive brands with "Only at Magnolia Road" printed on the label
  • No other menu carries them, so there is nothing to price-shop against
  • Convert today's Summit and Dro buyers into customers who come back for yours

Be a Destination

Driving Foot Traffic and Customer Retention
